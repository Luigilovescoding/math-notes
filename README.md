# Mathematical notes

Write-ups of problems I worked through on my own, from my time in French
*classes préparatoires* and my first years at École Polytechnique. The results
are classical; the write-ups and the routes taken are my own.

## Contents

**[Continuity of convex functions on Rⁿ](convex-continuity/continuity-convex-functions.pdf)** (2025)
A proof that a convex function on Rⁿ is continuous, by recursive two-point
bounding over the vertices of a hypercube. The standard route treats the cube as
the convex hull of its 2ⁿ vertices and applies Jensen's inequality; this one uses
nothing beyond the two-point definition of convexity, which keeps it within the
prépa syllabus.

**[Uniqueness of the Dunford decomposition](dunford/dunford.pdf)** (2024)
An independent solution showing that the semisimple part of S + N is a polynomial
in S + N, the polynomial being constructed by Hermite-type interpolation.
